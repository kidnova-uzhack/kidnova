About KidNova

One connected system around a child's screening journey.

KidNova is a pediatric HealthTech concept for kindergartens. It connects child-friendly screening, identity-linked records, parent communication and responsible human follow-up.

Created for UzHack 2026, the project begins with the needs of families and kindergartens in Fergana and is being developed through the Uzbekistan–Malaysia Tech Bridge.

MVP 2 · Telegram bot

KidNova for parents — right in Telegram.

@kidnova_uz_bot is a working Python bot. Parents keep a child profile and health diary, link their child to the kindergarten with a one-time code, see KidNova check results, ask an AI assistant and find a nearby clinic.

    MVP 3 · Smart Nutrition

    KidNova Smart Nutrition — smart nutrition oversight

    KidNova Smart Nutrition is a working calculator MVP that helps kindergarten kitchen staff estimate the calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat and fiber of one portion of a children's meal and plan how much food to buy. Saving daily menus is planned for the next stage.

    Working calculator MVP · approximate data · not yet used in a kindergarten

    4. 4

    Prototype integration

    Integration with KidNova RFID

    In the demo, the calculator can take the number of portions from today’s simulated RFID check-ins (staff demo account), so meal portions follow the number of children who arrived.

    1. RFID attendance
    2. Number of children
    3. Portion planning
    4. Nutrition report
    Simulated RFID data only. Attendance does not prove that a child actually ate the meal.
    Expected benefits

    Economic benefits for kindergartens

    • More accurate planningBetter planning of the food quantity needed, based on the number of children.
    • Less wasteHelping to reduce the amount of food prepared in excess.
    • Digital managementManaging daily menus and nutrition data in one system.
    Expected benefits — not experimentally verified. No savings figures are claimed.

    Planned requirements before any use

    Verified food dataFood-composition values suited to Uzbekistan, with recipe yield and cooking losses taken into account.
    Dietitian-approved standardsAge-appropriate nutrient targets reviewed by a qualified dietitian.
    Allergen awarenessAllergen information clearly labelled for authorized staff.
    Controlled accessA separate kitchen role with edit history; parent and staff access stay separate.

    Smart Nutrition is not a medical diagnosis tool, does not prescribe personalized diets and does not replace a qualified dietitian. It will require verified food-composition data and human review.

    Child safety, privacy & trust

    Trust must be designed before deployment.

    KidNova is intended to support supervised screening workflows — never autonomous diagnosis.

    ✓

    Consent & transparency

    Parent or guardian consent and clear information about how data is used.

    ⌾

    Restricted access

    Role-appropriate access, secure identification and future access logging.

    ◇

    Data responsibility

    Data minimization, responsible storage and appropriate retention policies.

    ♙

    Human supervision

    Trained personnel review screening context and coordinate appropriate follow-up.

    ⚙

    Hardware assurance

    Calibration, maintenance and reliability testing before any pilot use.

    Clinical Design Considerations

    Not attributed to the consulted pediatrician
    • Use non-contact infrared temperature screening only as an initial indicator.

    • Send alerts to responsible staff and parents through agreed workflows.
    • Monitor growth periodically using appropriate standards.
    • Protect privacy and obtain parental consent.
    • Do not produce automated medical diagnoses.
